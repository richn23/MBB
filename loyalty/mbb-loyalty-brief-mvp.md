# MBB Loyalty. Project brief (reset)

## What this is

A single page that explains MBB Loyalty: a way for companies to reward their best people with real experiences from luxury brands, while those brands get introduced to exactly the kind of new clients they want. It sits at meaningbeyondbrands.com/loyalty, separate from the main site, shared as a private link with the people you're pitching.

## Who sees it

Not the general public. It goes to two kinds of people: someone at a company who could put this in front of their team, and someone at a luxury brand who could take part. They'll likely be on their phone or laptop, reading it once, deciding if it's worth a conversation.

## What it needs to do

In under two minutes, a reader should understand what MBB Loyalty is, why it matters to them specifically, and feel it's credible enough to want to talk further. That's the whole job of the page. Everything else is in service of that.

## The MVP: what's essential

The page needs seven short sections, in this order. The copy for all of them is already written and signed off:

1. Opening: what MBB Loyalty is, in one line.
2. The problem: why companies struggle to keep their best people engaged, and why luxury brands struggle to reach the right new clients.
3. A worked example: what this actually looks like in practice, step by step.
4. How it works: the company, MBB, and the brand, and what each one gets out of it.
5. The point: why an experience is remembered when a bonus is forgotten.
6. Why MBB: the trust and relationships behind this, not just an idea.
7. Close: what MBB Loyalty is building, and a way to take the next step.

Beyond the words, the MVP needs: MBB's colours and fonts (sage, navy, gold, the same as the main site), a layout that looks good on both phone and laptop, quick loading, and one clear thing to do at the end (see "still open" below). That's it. That's a finished, sendable page.

## What's not needed for MVP

Everything we've spent the last several rounds building: the scroll animations, the layered 3D depth effect, pages transitioning in and out as you scroll. That's polish on top of a page that already works. It's worth coming back to once the plain version is signed off, but it shouldn't hold up getting something in front of people.

## What we already have

Copy: written and approved. Colours and fonts: chosen. Images: founder photo, emblem, compass mark, a couple of background textures. In short, the hard part (deciding what to say and how to say it) is done. What's left is mostly assembly.
